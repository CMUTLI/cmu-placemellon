Carnegie Mellon, Any Size

Put a PlaceMellon address in an image tag and get a CMU photo cropped to exactly that size. For mockups, prototypes and pages still waiting on their real photos.

Use It See the Options

Use It

Give the width and height you want, in pixels, after the address:

<img src="https://placemellon.andrew.cmu.edu/400/300" alt="">

For a square, give one number: /400 is 400 by 400. Sizes run from 1 to 2048.

  • The same size always gets the same photo, so your mockup looks the same every time you open it.
  • Wide sizes get landscape photos and tall sizes get portrait ones, so less is cropped away.
  • Photos are cropped around what's interesting in them, such as people and buildings.

Options

Add options after a ?, joined with &, in any order: /400/300?category=labs&gray.

OptionWhat It DoesExample
?category= Only photos from one category: campus, classrooms, labs or life.
?random A different photo every time. A page shows one image per address, so number them for several on one page: ?random=1, ?random=2.
?gray Grayscale. ?grayscale works too, and so does /g/ in front, as on placekitten: /g/400/300.
?scotty Scotty instead of a photo, standing on the bottom edge.
?profile Scotty as a round profile picture, for people who don't have a photo yet. Always square: /96?profile.

?category and ?random choose photos, so they don't go with ?scotty or ?profile. A misspelled or unknown option gets an error that lists the right ones, rather than being ignored.

About the Photos

The photos come from CMU Selects, University Communications & Marketing's collection of campus photography. CMU Selects says they're for "use by the Carnegie Mellon University community in university-related communications and marketing," so use PlaceMellon for CMU work. For a finished page, choose and credit a photo there.

Scotty comes from the Office of Human Resources' Scotty avatar.

Which Photo Is This?

Each photo's response has an X-Photo header with its file name, so you can find it in CMU Selects.
